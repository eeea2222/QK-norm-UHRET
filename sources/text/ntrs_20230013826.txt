What does this mean for future Lunar habitats?
• In Lunar gravity, even very low forced flow velocities can disrupt smoke layer formation
• Smoke detectors could be placed on ceilings to facilitate earlier smoke detection, but detector
placement relative to supplies and returns must be carefully considered
What are the next steps?
• Evaluate different supply/return placements and range of tested flow velocities
• How does smoke layer development change?
• Is there an alternative to ceiling/floor placement?
• Simplifying assumptions about particle transport must be considered with each scenario
• Explore different, more realistic early fire scenarios
